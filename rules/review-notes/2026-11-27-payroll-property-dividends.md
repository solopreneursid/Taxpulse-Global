# Scoped payroll, property and dividend tax source review — 27 November 2026
Preliminary source cross-check only; production manifest unchanged.
- IRS Publication 15 and Topic 751: https://www.irs.gov/publications/p15 and https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc751. Calendar 2026 employer Social Security 6.2% on first USD 184,500; employer Medicare 1.45% on all covered wages. USD 200k yields 11,439 + 2,900 = 14,339. No employer Additional Medicare Tax. Aggregate annual arithmetic only, not individual payroll rounding or total employment cost.
- IRAS BSD: https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/stamp-duty/for-property/buying-or-acquiring-property/buyer's-stamp-duty-(bsd). From 15 February 2023, residential bands 1/2/3/4/5/6% at 180k, 360k, 1m, 1.5m, 3m; nonresidential maximum 5%. Higher of price and established value, floor duty to dollar, minimum 1. Official residential example SGD 4,500,100 yields 209,606. Excludes ABSD, SSD, mixed use, valuation and remission.
- HMRC https://www.gov.uk/tax-on-dividends and Finance Act 2026 https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2026/11/part/1/crossheading/income-tax-charge-rates-and-allowances/made. 2026–27 ordinary dividend rates 10.75/35.75/39.35%, 500 allowance. Other taxable income and taxable dividends already established after final Personal Allowance allocation, no savings income, special cases or band extensions. Dividend allowance consumes bands. HMRC example 17k other taxable income and 3k dividends gives 2,500 × 10.75% = 268.75. At 37k other + 1k dividends, 500 allowance, remaining 200 basic and 300 higher yields 128.75. Component only, not total tax.
