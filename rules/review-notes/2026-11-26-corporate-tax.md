# Scoped company-tax source review — 2026-11-26
Not independent statutory approval. No legacy corporate module reactivated. Narrow calculation on already established taxable profits only.

- UK 2026 financial year: 19% small profits, 25% main rate, marginal relief fraction 3/200 (Finance Act 2025 sections 13–14; https://www.legislation.gov.uk/ukpga/2025/8/body/enacted ; https://www.gov.uk/corporation-tax-rates ; https://www.gov.uk/guidance/corporation-tax-marginal-relief). Scope is a sole eligible resident company with full aligned period, ordinary non-ring-fence profits, no associated companies, no exempt distributions, augmented profits equal taxable profits. Therefore marginal relief between 50k and 250k is (250k − taxable profit) × 3/200. Independently derived examples: 50k × .19 = 9,500; 100k × .25 − 150k × .015 = 22,750; 250k × .25 = 62,500.
- UAE resident ordinary juridical person: 0% on first AED 375k, 9% on excess (https://mof.gov.ae/en/news/ministry-of-finance-confirms-applicable-taxable-income-threshold-for-corporate-tax-abu-dhabi-uae/ ; https://tax.gov.ae/en/faq.aspx?keyword=if+a+business+has+earned+taxable+income+of+aed+1+million%2C+what+will+be+the+uae+ct+amount+payable). AED 1m produces AED 56,250; AED 375,100 produces AED 9. Exclude special free-zone, small business relief, multinational minimum-tax and exempt entities.
- AU FY2026–27 ordinary resident company: 25% for confirmed eligible base rate entity or 30% otherwise, Income Tax Rates Act 1986 as compiled 1 July 2026 (https://www.legislation.gov.au/C2004A03348/2026-07-01/2026-07-01/text/original/epub/OEBPS/document_1/document_1.html ; https://www.ato.gov.au/tax-rates-and-codes/company-tax-rate-changes). 100k yields 25k or 30k respectively. No rate eligibility inferred.
- Other relief, credits, withholding, corporate residency, taxable-profit adjustments, actual filing rounding, accounting periods outside this scope and dividend taxation not modeled. Nearest minor-unit example arithmetic. Full statutory review remains pending.
